**Wear Active – Shipping Policy**

At Wear Active, we deliver across Pakistan with free standard shipping on every order.

### Delivery coverage and charges

Wear Active provides **free standard delivery across Pakistan** with no minimum order value.

### Delivery time

Orders are normally delivered within **2–5 working days**. This timeframe includes order processing and delivery.

Delivery may take longer during public holidays, major sales, severe weather, courier disruptions, or for locations outside standard courier service areas.

### Courier and tracking

Orders are delivered through **Leopards** or **Trax**.

Once your order is dispatched, tracking details are shared with you on **WhatsApp**.

### Cash on Delivery confirmation

Cash on Delivery orders must be confirmed through WhatsApp before dispatch. If we cannot confirm your order, dispatch may be delayed.

### Payment methods

Wear Active accepts:

- Cash on Delivery
- Bank Transfer
- JazzCash

For Bank Transfer and JazzCash, follow the payment instructions provided during checkout or order confirmation.

Wear Active will never ask for your banking password, card PIN, JazzCash PIN, or one-time password. If you receive a suspicious payment request, contact us directly.

### Address and delivery issues

You are responsible for providing a complete and accurate name, phone number, and delivery address at checkout.

If you need to correct an address, have not received tracking details, or have a delivery issue, contact us as soon as possible:

- **WhatsApp:** +92 314 0877221 (preferred)
- **Email:** info@wearactive.pk

### Order processing

Orders are processed on working days (Monday–Saturday). Orders placed after our daily cut-off, on Sundays, or on public holidays are processed on the next working day.

We may contact you to confirm order or payment details before dispatch, especially for Cash on Delivery orders.

### Failed or refused delivery

If a delivery cannot be completed because of an incorrect address, an unreachable phone number, or refusal to accept the parcel, the order may be returned to us. Any re-delivery costs may be charged to the customer.

### Size exchanges and returns

For size exchanges, returns, and refunds, see our **Returns & Exchange Policy**.

Size exchanges are available within 7 days of delivery, including discounted products. A Rs. 300 exchange delivery fee applies to size exchanges. If Wear Active sends an incorrect or defective item, replacement delivery is free.

### Contact us

For shipping and delivery questions:

- **WhatsApp:** +92 314 0877221
- **Email:** info@wearactive.pk
- **Hours:** 12 PM–8 PM, Monday–Saturday
- **Typical response time:** Within 30 minutes during support hours

**Wear Active – Returns & Exchange Policy**

At Wear Active, we want you to love your purchase. If something is not right, please review the policy below.

### 7-Day Size Exchange

Size exchanges are available within 7 days of delivery, including discounted products.

A Rs. 300 exchange delivery fee applies.

Items must be unused, unworn, unwashed, and have original tags attached.

If Wear Active sends an incorrect or defective item, replacement delivery is free.

We do not offer returns or refunds for a change of mind.

### Returns and refunds (faulty, defective, or incorrect items)

Returns and refunds may be available within 7 days of delivery when:

- The item is faulty, damaged, or defective at delivery.
- The item does not match the original specifications or the item you ordered.

For faulty or defective items, contact us with photographic evidence within 7 days.

Original delivery and shipping charges are non-refundable. For approved refunds, Rs. 250 is deducted to cover delivery charges.

Refunds are processed within 7 working days after we receive and verify returned items. Normal processing time is 2–4 business days; during sales it may take 6–8 business days.

### Item condition

All returned or exchanged items must be:

- Unworn, unwashed, undamaged, and with all original tags attached.
- Free of odours, signs of wear, or tampering.
- In their original condition.

Discounted products are eligible for size exchange within 7 days, subject to the conditions above.

### How to start an exchange or return

Contact us within 7 days of delivery:

- **WhatsApp:** +92 314 0877221 (preferred)
- **Email:** info@wearactive.pk

Include your order number, the reason for your request, and—for size exchanges—the replacement size required. WhatsApp or email confirmation serves as proof of your request.

Return items via a traceable courier or registered post at your cost to our returns address (provided upon request). The parcel remains your responsibility until received and processed. Please keep proof of postage.

Exchanges are shipped on standard service (not express).

### Faulty goods

For suspected faults, contact us with a brief description and clear photographs.

In some cases, returning the item may not be necessary; we assess each case individually.

Faulty claims should be made within 4–7 days of delivery. After this period, issues may be considered normal wear and tear.

If a like-for-like replacement is out of stock, we may issue a refund or store credit equal to the product's value.

### When returns or exchanges are not possible

- Requests made outside the 7-day timeframe.
- Products damaged due to use, mishandling, or tampering.
- Items that do not meet the condition requirements above.
- Missing details for the required exchange size or item.

### Important notes

- We reserve the right to return items to you if they do not meet our policy criteria.
- Goods are returned to you in the same condition in which they are received.
- Exchanges can only be made up to the original value of the goods purchased.
- Your original shipping costs are non-refundable.

### Contact us

For questions about exchanges and returns:

- **WhatsApp:** +92 314 0877221
- **Email:** info@wearactive.pk
- **Hours:** 12 PM–8 PM, Monday–Saturday
- **Typical response time:** Within 30 minutes during support hours
