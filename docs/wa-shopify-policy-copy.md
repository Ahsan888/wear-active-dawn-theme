# Wear Active — Shopify policy copy

Use this approved copy in **Shopify Admin → Settings → Policies**. Review it whenever the delivery, exchange, courier, payment, or support process changes.

## Shipping policy

### Delivery coverage and charges

Wear Active provides free standard delivery across Pakistan with no minimum order value.

### Delivery time

Orders are normally delivered within 2–5 working days. This timeframe includes order processing and delivery. Delivery may take longer during public holidays, major sales, severe weather, courier disruptions, or for locations outside standard courier service areas.

### Courier and tracking

Orders are delivered through Leopards or Trax. Once an order is dispatched, its tracking details are shared with the customer on WhatsApp.

### Cash on Delivery confirmation

Cash on Delivery orders must be confirmed through WhatsApp before dispatch. If we cannot confirm an order, dispatch may be delayed.

### Address and delivery issues

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate name, phone number, and delivery address. If you need to correct an address or have not received tracking details, contact us on WhatsApp at +92 314 0877221 as soon as possible.

Support is available from 12 PM to 8 PM, Monday to Saturday. We usually reply within 30 minutes during support hours.

## Size exchange policy

Wear Active offers size exchanges within 7 days of delivery, including for discounted products. We currently provide size exchanges only and do not offer returns or refunds for a change of mind.

### Exchange eligibility

To qualify for a size exchange, the item must:

- Be unused and unwashed.
- Have its original tags attached.
- Be in its original condition.
- Be requested within 7 days of delivery.

A fixed exchange delivery fee of Rs. 300 applies.

### Incorrect or defective items

If Wear Active sends an incorrect item or the item arrives defective, Wear Active covers the delivery charges. Contact us on WhatsApp with your order number and clear photographs of the item so our team can review and resolve the issue.

### Starting an exchange

Message Wear Active on WhatsApp at +92 314 0877221 with:

- Your order number.
- The product and size received.
- The replacement size required.
- Photographs if the item is incorrect or defective.

Support is available from 12 PM to 8 PM, Monday to Saturday. We usually reply within 30 minutes during support hours.

## Payment information

Wear Active accepts:

- Cash on Delivery.
- Bank Transfer.
- JazzCash.

Cash on Delivery orders are confirmed through WhatsApp before dispatch. For Bank Transfer and JazzCash, follow the payment instructions provided by Wear Active during checkout or order confirmation.

Wear Active will never ask for your banking password, card PIN, JazzCash PIN, or one-time password. If you receive a suspicious payment request, contact us directly on WhatsApp at +92 314 0877221 or email info@wearactive.pk.
